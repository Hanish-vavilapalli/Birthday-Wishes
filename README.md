# Nanna's Love Letter

Yes. Then the PRD should focus less on “features” and more on making her feel the story while scrolling. Still keep it clean and not overloaded.

❤️ PRD: Happiest Birthday Nanna

1. Concept

A deeply emotional birthday landing page created as a personal surprise for Nanna.

The experience should feel like opening a private love letter, where every scroll reveals another feeling, memory, or message.

The website should never feel like a normal portfolio or commercial landing page.

Core emotion:
Love → Memories → Gratitude → Birthday Wish → Heartfelt Message

2. Hero: Happiest Birthday ❤️

The screen starts almost completely dark.

A very soft glow slowly appears.

Small particles and a few subtle hearts begin floating.

Then the text slowly appears:

Happiest Birthday, Nanna ❤️

After a short pause:

“If I could give you one thing today,
it would be the ability to see yourself
through my eyes.”

The text should appear slowly, giving the visitor time to read and feel it.

3. Our Story

Transition naturally from the hero.

Heading:

“And then, there was us…”

A short personal story appears gradually.

The writing should talk about:

How you met

How you became close

Small moments

How she became important to you

Don't make it a huge paragraph.

Use several short emotional lines so each one can appear individually.

Example:

“I didn't know that an ordinary day
would eventually become one of the most important parts of my life.”

4. The Little Things

A very minimal section about the things you love about her.

Instead of a list of 20 things, reveal 4–5 meaningful moments.

For example:

Your smile.
The one that can change my entire mood.

Your voice.
Somehow, it always feels like home.

The way you care.
Even about the smallest things.

Each statement appears one at a time while scrolling.

5. Our Memories 📸

The memories section should feel like opening an old photo album.

Photos appear individually rather than as a standard grid.

Each photo can have a tiny caption:

“That day ❤️”

“One of my favorite memories.”

“I wish I could go back to this moment.”

The photos should use slow zoom, fade and parallax effects.

6. A Moment To Pause

After the memories, slow everything down.

The screen becomes quieter.

Only a soft background glow remains.

Text appears:

“Some memories become photographs.
Some become a part of us.”

Then:

“You became both.”

Pause before continuing.

This section is intentionally simple.

7. Birthday Message 💌

Main emotional section.

Heading:

“Nanna, this is for you…”

Your personal birthday letter appears gradually.

The page should give the feeling of actually reading a letter.

Possible opening:

“Happiest Birthday to the person who means more to me than I can put into words…”

The rest of the message should be completely personal and written in your own words.

No unnecessary UI around it.

8. Emotional Ending

There should not be a separate “Final” section.

Instead, after the birthday message finishes, the page should simply become quieter.

The last line of your message remains visible.

The background glow continues very softly.

A few tiny particles/hearts keep moving.

Then the page naturally ends.

🎨 Visual Direction

Background

A single continuous romantic environment throughout the website:

Deep dark background

Soft pink/rose glow

Subtle light particles

Very few floating hearts

Gentle vignette

No hard section boundaries.

Typography

Use elegant typography with plenty of empty space.

Important emotional sentences can be larger than normal text.

✨ Animation Philosophy

Every animation should have a reason.

Slow → Soft → Emotional → Cinematic

Use:

Text reveal

Fade-in

Slow zoom

Parallax

Floating particles

Soft heart animation

Gentle light movement

Smooth scrolling

Cross-section transitions

Avoid anything that makes the page feel childish or flashy.

📱 Mobile Experience

The emotional experience must work especially well on mobile.

Full-screen hero

Large readable text

One memory at a time where appropriate

Smooth touch scrolling

Proper image cropping

No horizontal scrolling

Fast loading

🧩 Final Flow

Happiest Birthday, Nanna ❤️
          ↓
   “And then, there was us…”
          ↓
    Our Little Things
          ↓
      Our Memories
          ↓
    A Moment To Pause
          ↓
   “Nanna, this is for you…”
          ↓
      Personal Letter
          ↓
      Natural Ending

The key idea

Don't try to make her impressed by the website. Make her forget she's looking at a website.

It should feel like you are talking directly to her through the screen. ❤️
